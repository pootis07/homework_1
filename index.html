Burger Recipe Pizza Recipe

🍔 Homemade Cheeseburger

Juicy beef, melted cheese, and fresh toppings.

Prep: 15 minutes | Cook: 10 minutes

Ingredients (4 burgers)

  • 500 g ground beef
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tbsp ketchup
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 4 burger buns
  • 4 slices of cheese
  • Lettuce, tomato, pickles and onion

Instructions

  1. Mix the beef, Worcestershire sauce, ketchup, garlic powder, onion powder and pepper gently.
  2. Shape into four patties and season with salt.
  3. Cook in a hot skillet, flipping as needed, until the centers reach 71°C (160°F).
  4. Add cheese near the end and let it melt.
  5. Toast the buns and add your favorite toppings.

🍕 Easy Homemade Pizza

Crispy crust, tomato sauce and stretchy cheese!

Prep: 20 minutes | Bake: 12–15 minutes

Ingredients (2 small pizzas)

  • 300 g all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp instant yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 180 ml warm water
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 tbsp pizza sauce or tomato sauce
  • 200 g shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Pepperoni, mushrooms or bell peppers (optional)
  • Dried oregano (optional)

Instructions

  1. Mix flour, yeast, sugar and salt in a bowl.
  2. Add warm water and olive oil. Knead for 5–8 minutes until the dough is smooth.
  3. Cover and let the dough rise for about 45–60 minutes, until puffed up.
  4. Preheat the oven to 240°C (465°F), or as hot as your oven safely allows.
  5. Divide the dough into two pieces and roll them into pizza bases.
  6. Spread tomato sauce over each base. Add cheese and your favorite toppings.
  7. Bake for 12–15 minutes, until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbling.
  8. Let the pizzas cool slightly, slice and enjoy!
